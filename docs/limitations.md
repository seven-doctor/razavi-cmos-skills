# Limitations

1. OCR can corrupt signs, subscripts, superscripts, Greek letters, equations, tables, and circuit diagrams.
2. A chapter summary is a navigational and study aid, not a verbatim transcription or proof certificate.
3. The answer skill is a solution workflow and validation aid; it is not a guarantee that every answer-book line has been reproduced.
4. Numerical design decisions require original-page verification and, where relevant, SPICE or equivalent simulation.
5. The materials do not replace a foundry PDK, design-rule manual, reliability limits, or engineering review.
